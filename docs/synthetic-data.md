## Synthetic Data Strategy

This synthetic module is designed to generate a coherent neurorehabilitation story rather than a statistically validated population model. The exact values, branching rules, and outcome scores are illustrative defaults that may be replaced with distributions, randomized variation, or expert-calibrated rules in future iterations.

The module should be used to support development, sandbox testing, and workflow demonstration. It does not represent a clinical recommendation engine and should not be used as evidence of treatment efficacy or patient suitability.

The current synthetic cohort is intended to provide a believable longitudinal pathway covering stroke onset, rehabilitation routing, physiological monitoring, session documentation, and outcome tracking. Its role is to make the app easier to build, test, and explain, not to model the full complexity of real-world recovery.
